# Product scope and assumptions

This repository describes a general-purpose AI mail assistant for insurance account managers. It does not define the procedures of any particular insurer, broker or customer.

The [story catalogue](user-stories.md) defines generic proposed behavior. Initial scenarios cover renewal coordination, local periodic report delivery, program changes, email understanding and controlled replies. Additional reporting and meeting workflows are deferred extensions.

Roles such as account manager, broker, underwriting team, reporting team and local team describe configurable responsibilities. Every deployment must define its own authorized sources, recipients, completion evidence, calendars, lead times and approval policy. Examples are synthetic; illustrative values are not contractual rules.

Capability specs define acceptance contracts. Design documents select technical approaches. The decision register identifies unresolved deployment and business policies. A demo, generated draft or passing fixture cannot establish business authority or production readiness.

Only generic documentation and declared development-tool dependencies belong in this repository. Do not add customer correspondence, source business documents, internal reviews, tenant identifiers, private endpoints, credentials or production data.
